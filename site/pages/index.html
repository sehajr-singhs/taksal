A research home

Gurbani and Sikh history, searchable and citable.

Taksal brings Gurbani, the Dasam Granth, Vaaran, historical texts, hukamnamas, digital pothis, translations and scholarship into one place. Every line can be read in the form you need and cited exactly.

The idea came from reading Sicques, Tigers, or Thieves, edited by Amandeep Singh Madra and Parmjit Singh, and seeing how long the authors spent finding and translating sources. Taksal is meant to make that kind of research faster for everyone.

The plan

An open corpus, read closely.

Taksal is building an open corpus of Sikh texts, an OCR pipeline for old manuscripts, and a model that answers only with citations to the sources on this site. The reader and this catalog are the first public pieces.

Taksal is independent and not affiliated with Damdami Taksal or any jatha.
