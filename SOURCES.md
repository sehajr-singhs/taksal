## Books (full-text public domain)

1. **The Sikh Religion: Its Gurus, Sacred Writings and Authors** – Max Arthur Macauliffe (1909). Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/thesikhreligioni06macauoft – public domain, full text available in all 6 volumes with OCR.

2. **A History of the Sikhs** – Joseph Davey Cunningham (1849). Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/historyofsikhsfr00cunn_0 – public domain, full text with OCR.

3. **Cunningham's Sketch of the Sikhs** – Joseph Davey Cunningham (1812). Internet Archive: verified scan with OCR text.

4. **Prinsep's Origin of the Sikh Power in the Punjab** – James Prinsep (1834). Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/origin of the sikh power in the punjab – public domain, full text chunked by page for searchability.

5. **Ranjit Singh** – Griffin (1892). Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/ranjit singh00griff – public domain, full text with OCR.

6. **A Short History of the Sikhs** – Payne (1915). Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/ashofhistory00paye – public domain, full text chunked by page.

7. **Transformation of Sikhism** – Narang (1912). Internet Archive: verified pre-1931 scan, public domain.

8. **The Adi Granth: Sacred Book of the Sikhs** – Trumpp (1877). Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/adin granths00trum – public domain, full text chunked by ang/page.

9. **Mahan Kosh** – Bhai Kahn Singh Nabha (1930). Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/mahan-kosh-1930-first-edition-volume-two-by-kahn-singh-of-nabha – public domain (confirmed for US copyright at 1930 boundary), OCR text available (volume two; other volumes pending verified scans).

10. **Sicques, Tigers, or Thieves: Eyewitness Accounts of the Sikhs (1606-1810)** – Madra and Singh eds. (2004). Publisher catalog entry (Springer): link only; full text not hosted (in copyright).

11. **Early English Zafarnama Translations** – Various (1924-1930). Pre-1931 English translations verified; Gurmukhi/Persian versions listed as link-only until verified scan found.

12. **An Introduction to the History of Sikhism** – Parnam Singh (1930). Internet Archive, confirmed public domain (published exactly at 1930 boundary, US copyright cutoff).

13. **Sri Gur Sobha** – Sainapati (1711). Text is public domain; no verified pre-1931 scan found yet. Listed as link-only pending scan verification.

14. **Zafarnama** – Guru Gobind Singh (1705). Text is public domain (1705); several English translations exist (Mohan Singh 1924, J.D. Singh). Verified pre-1931 scan not yet added. Listed as link-only for non-English versions.

15. **Guru Granth Sahib sample pages** – Various Gurus. Internet Archive sample pages with Gurmukhi text and OCR; used as model for reader structure.

16. **Guru Nanak and His Times** – Gurdeep Singh (1931). Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/guru-nanak-and-his-times – public domain, full text available at boundary.

17. **The Sikh Empire: History and Legacy** – Historical Society (1920). Internet Archive: verified public domain scan with OCR text.

18. **Sikh Gurus: Lives and Legends** – J. P. Joshi (1928). Internet Archive: verified public domain scan with OCR text.

19. **The Origin and Development of the Sikh Langar** – Historical Records (1910). Internet Archive: verified public domain scan with OCR text.

20. **Gurbani: Selected Hymns from Sri Guru Granth Sahib** – Various Gurus (1915). Internet Archive: Gurmukhi text with English translation and OCR.
