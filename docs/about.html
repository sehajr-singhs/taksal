About

The Sikh today has a struggle to get any in-depth research on Gurbani, history or Sikhi. Taksal is a way to make it better and easier.

The idea came from reading Sicques, Tigers, or Thieves, and seeing how much of the record is scattered across old books and archives that are hard to find and harder to search.

The plan: an open library of sources, OCR for old manuscripts, and a model that answers only with citations.

Taksal is independent and not affiliated with Damdami Taksal or any jatha.
