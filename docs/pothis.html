Digitized pothis coming. Help transcribe.

What goes here

Pothis and bir saroops from private collections and gurdwaras, photographed at high resolution, with a transcription aligned to each page. Transcriptions will be reviewed by more than one reader before they are published. If you hold a manuscript and want it photographed, or you want to help transcribe, reach out to the team.

How the pothi viewer works

  1. Left panel: High-resolution scan of a pothi page from Wikimedia Commons
  2. Right panel: Line-by-line transcription with Gurmukhi, Larivaar, English and transliteration
  3. Navigation: Next/previous buttons to move through a pothi's pages
  4. Metadata: Source URL, author, license, and digitization details

Note: Actual images are linked to their Wikimedia Commons file pages. Taksal does not host or download any manuscript images.
